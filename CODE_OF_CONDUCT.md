# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Treat everyone in the project with respect, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its standard of behaviour.

Expected behaviour:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful toward others.
- Welcoming differing viewpoints and accepting constructive feedback.
- Keeping the community's and project's interests in mind.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

The full, authoritative text is in the linked Contributor Covenant.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer,
[@KiwiGaze](https://github.com/KiwiGaze). For sensitive reports, use the
repository's [private reporting](https://github.com/KiwiGaze/glm-for-copilot/security/advisories/new)
channel. The maintainer will review reports promptly and fairly and protect the reporter's
privacy.
